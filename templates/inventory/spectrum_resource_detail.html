Why this record exists

Frequency reuse is not decided by Beam identity. Allocations compete when they occupy the same physical or logical resource — a shared RF chain, or a shared satellite payload input.

Gateway, Hub, antenna and site identity do not create a separate resource. Two redundant antennas at different sites feeding the same payload input are one resource, and allocations through them compete.
